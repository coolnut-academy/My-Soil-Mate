/// MULTIPLE LINEAR REGRESSION ///

$$ Score = \beta_0 + (\beta_1 \times pH) + (\beta_2 \times Temp) + (\beta_3 \times Rain) + (\beta_4 \times Solar) $$

ค่าสัมประสิทธิ์ (Coefficients)

  • Intercept (\(\beta_0\)) -53.527421
  • pH Weight (\(\beta_1\)) +3.021859
  • Temp Weight (\(\beta_2\)) +3.294926
  • Rain Weight (\(\beta_3\)) +0.106848
  • Solar Weight (\(\beta_4\)) +0.567525
